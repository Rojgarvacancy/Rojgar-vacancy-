Social Welfare Department, Uttar Pradesh (UP SWD)
UP Scholarship Post Matric & Dashmottar Online Form 2026-2027
Short Information : UP SWD Social Welfare Department, Uttar Pradesh has released the notification for Post Matric Scholarship 2026-2027. Candidates who are studying in Class 11, 12 or Other Dashmottar Courses (UG, PG, Diploma, Certificate) in Uttar Pradesh schools, colleges, or universities can apply online for Fresh and Renewal scholarship applications from 22/07/2026. Read the notification for complete guidelines, eligibility, and document requirements.
Important Dates Application Fee
• Application Begin : 22/07/2026
• Renewal Candidate Last Date : 15/10/2026
• Fresh Candidate Last Date : 31/10/2026
• Hard Copy Submit to College Last Date : As per Schedule
• Correction Date : As Per Schedule
• Correction Last Date : As per Schedule 		• General / OBC / EWS : ₹ 0/-
• SC / ST / PH : ₹ 0/-

No Application Fee for All Candidates, Only Fill Online Form.
UP Scholarship Eligibility 2026-2027 Documents Required (Fresh Candidates)
• Uttar Pradesh Enrolled with School, College, Institution or University.

• Post Matric 11 : Passed Class 10 Exam and Enrolled in Class 11.
• Post Matric 12 : Passed Class 11 Exam and Enrolled in Class 12.
• Dashmottar : Under Graduate / Post Graduate / Certificate / Diploma Courses. 		• Last Qualifying Exam Mark Sheet
• Caste Certificate
• Income Certificate
• Bank Passbook
• Fee Receipt Number
• Annual Non Refundable Amount
• Enrollment Number
• Aadhar Card Number (Compulsory)
• Latest Passport Size Scanned Photo

For Renewal Candidates : Kindly Use Last Year Registration Number to Login to Renewal Section and Enter the Fresh Details.

How to Apply Uttar Pradesh UP Scholarship 2026-2027 Online Form

• Fresh Candidates : Whoever is a new candidate who has taken admission this year in Class 11, 12 (High School / Intermediate) & Other Dashmottar Courses (UG / PG / Diploma / Certificate) should apply under Fresh.
• Renewal Candidates : Candidates who are already enrolled in any school / college and applied for scholarships last year will renew their application.
• Kindly collect all documents before applying online.
• If you do not know the non-refundable amount, please check your fee receipt OR contact your college/school.
• Submit the hard copy of your form to the concerned school/college within 3 days of the completion of the application.
• Aadhar Card is compulsory for the Uttar Pradesh UP Scholarship Application 2026-2027.
