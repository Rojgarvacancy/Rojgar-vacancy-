  <h3 style="background: rgb(21, 101, 192); color: white; font-size: 16px; margin: 0px; padding: 8px 12px;">How to Download MPESB Group-2 Sub Group-4 Admit Card</h3>
  <div style="background: #ffffff; border: 1px solid rgb(144, 202, 249); margin-bottom: 15px; padding: 10px 15px; font-size: 14px;">
    • Click on the <strong>Download Admit Card</strong> link given in the Important Links table below.<br>
    • Enter your 13-digit Application Number, Date of Birth (DD/MM/YYYY), and solve the captcha.<br>
    • Select your first 2 letters of Mother's Name + Last 4 digits of your Aadhaar Number (if prompted).<br>
    • Click on the <strong>Search</strong> button to view your Test Admit Card (TAC).<br>
    • Download and print a clear color or black & white copy of your admit card to carry to the exam center.
