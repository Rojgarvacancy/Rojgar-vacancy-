IAF Agniveervayu 02/2027 : Eligibility Details

Stream / Group Eligibility Criteria
Science Subjects
(Intake 02/2027) 		• 10+2 Intermediate with Mathematics, Physics, and English with minimum 50% marks in aggregate and 50% marks in English.
• OR Passed 3 Years Diploma Course in Engineering (Mechanical / Electrical / Electronics / Automobile / Computer Science / Instrumentation / IT) with 50% aggregate and 50% in English.
• OR Passed 2 Years Vocational Course with Non-Vocational Subjects Physics and Math with 50% aggregate and 50% in English.
Other Than Science Subjects • 10+2 Intermediate / Equivalent examination in any stream / subjects with minimum 50% marks in aggregate and 50% marks in English.
• OR Passed 2 Years Vocational Course with minimum 50% aggregate and 50% in English.

Physical Fitness Test (PFT) & Medical Standards

• Height : Male: Minimum 152.5 cms | Female: Minimum 152 cms (Relaxation applicable for North-East & Hilly areas).
• Chest Expansion : Minimum expansion of 05 cms.
• Running (1.6 Km) : Male candidates completed in 07 minutes | Female candidates completed in 08 minutes.
• Other Exercises (Male) : 10 Push-ups (01 min), 10 Sit-ups (01 min), 20 Squats (01 min).
• Other Exercises (Female) : 10 Sit-ups (01 min 30 sec), 15 Squats (01 min).

Indian Air Force Agniveer Package / Benefits

• 1st Year : ₹ 30,000/- per month (In-Hand : ₹ 21,000/-)
• 2nd Year : ₹ 33,000/- per month (In-Hand : ₹ 23,100/-)
• 3rd Year : ₹ 36,500/- per month (In-Hand : ₹ 25,580/-)
• 4th Year : ₹ 40,000/- per month (In-Hand : ₹ 28,000/-)
• Exit After 4 Years : ₹ 11.71 Lakh as Seva Nidhi Package + Skill Gained Certificate.
• Up to 25% of candidates will be enrolled in the regular cadre of the Indian Air Force.
