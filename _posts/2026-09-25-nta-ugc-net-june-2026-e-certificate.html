NTA UGC NET June 2026 : Educational Eligibility

Category / Mode Eligibility Criteria
Master's Degree (PG) • Passed OR Appeared in the Final Year of Master’s Degree in concerned/related subject from any recognized University.
• Minimum Marks Required : 55% Marks for General/EWS candidates and 50% Marks for SC/ST/OBC-NCL/PwD candidates.
4-Year Bachelor's Degree • Candidates with a 4-Year / 8-Semester Bachelor’s Degree programme with minimum 75% marks in aggregate (or its equivalent grade) are also eligible to apply for JRF and Ph.D admission.
Certificate Validity • JRF Award Letter Validity : 3 Years
• Assistant Professor Eligibility : Lifetime Validity

How to Download NTA UGC NET E-Certificate & Score Card 2026

• Click on the "Download E-Certificate" link given in the Important Links table below.
• Select the Institution (UGC NET) and enter the Captcha code to proceed.
• Enter your Application Number / Roll Number and Date of Birth (DOB).
• Click on Submit or Login.
• Your UGC NET E-Certificate and JRF Award Letter will be displayed on screen.
• Download the PDF file and keep multiple printouts for future academic and recruitment processes.
