Fisheries Extension Officer (मत्स्य प्रसार पदाधिकारी) Recruitment 2026 | Advt. No. 28/2026
Short Information : Bihar Technical Service Commission (BTSC) has invited online applications for the regular recruitment of Fisheries Extension Officer (मत्स्य प्रसार पदाधिकारी) under the Directorate of Fisheries, Dairy, Fisheries and Animal Resources Department, Govt. of Bihar.
