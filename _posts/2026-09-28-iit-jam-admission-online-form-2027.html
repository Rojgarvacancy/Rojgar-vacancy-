Joint Admission Test for Masters (IIT JAM 2027) Admission Online Form
Short Information : Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) has officially released the detailed information brochure and opened online applications for the Joint Admission Test for Masters (IIT JAM 2027). IIT JAM is conducted for admission into M.Sc., M.Sc. (Tech.), M.S. (Research), M.Sc.-M.Tech. Dual Degree, and Integrated Ph.D. Degree programs at premier IITs and IISc Bangalore. Eligible candidates can submit their online application forms before 11/10/2026. Check complete eligibility criteria, application fees, test papers, and direct links below.

How to Fill IIT JAM 2027 Online Application Form

• Visit the official IIT JAM portal by clicking the "Apply Online" link below.
• Register as a new user by providing a valid Mobile Number and Email ID to generate your enrollment credentials.
• Log in and select the test paper(s) (One or Two papers) and choose preferred exam examination cities.
• Fill in candidate personal details, academic qualifications, and communication address.
• Upload required documents including candidate photograph, signature, and category/PwD certificate (if applicable).
• Pay the requisite application fee via online mode and submit the final form. Keep a printed copy of the confirmation page for future reference.
