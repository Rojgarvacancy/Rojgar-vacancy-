  <h3>How to Check SSC CGL 2026 Tier 1 Exam City & Admit Card</h3>
  <ol>
    <li>Visit the official Staff Selection Commission portal via the login link given below.</li>
    <li>Log in using your <strong>Registration Number</strong> and <strong>Password</strong> generated during the OTR process.</li>
    <li>Navigate to the <strong>Admit Card / Exam City Dashboard</strong>.</li>
    <li>Check your examination city, shift timings, and reporting time.</li>
    <li>Download the official admit card PDF when released (4 days prior to your exam date) and take a clear printout to carry to the exam center along with a valid original photo ID.</li>
  </ol>
