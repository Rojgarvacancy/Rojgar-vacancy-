Canara Bank Graduate Apprentice Recruitment 2026 (Advt No. CB/RP/1/2026)
Short Information : Canara Bank has officially released the detailed notification for the recruitment of Graduate Apprentice under the Apprentices Act, 1961 for FY 2026-27 across various states in India for a total of 3500 Posts. Candidates who possess a recognized Bachelor's Degree and meet the eligibility criteria can apply online from 01/10/2026 to 17/10/2026. How to Fill Canara Bank Graduate Apprentice Online Form 2026

• First, visit the NATS 2.0 Portal and complete your student registration with a 100% verified profile.
• Keep your 12-digit NATS Student Enrollment ID handy before applying.
• Click on the "Apply Online" link below (available from 01/10/2026 to 17/10/2026) to open the Canara Bank Recruitment Portal.
• Fill in your personal details, educational qualifications, marks, and select your state/local language preferences.
• Upload required documents including photo, signature, and educational certificates as per specifications.
• Pay the required application fee (if applicable) online and submit the final form. Take a printout for future reference.
