  <!-- How to Fill Online Form Steps -->
  <h3 style="background: rgb(21, 101, 192); color: #ffffff; font-size: 15px; margin: 0; padding: 8px 12px;">How to Fill IIT GATE 2027 Online Application Form</h3>
  <div style="background: #ffffff; border: 1px solid #90caf9; margin-bottom: 15px; padding: 10px 14px; font-size: 13.5px;">
    • Niche diye gaye <strong>Apply Online (GOAPS Login)</strong> link par click karein.<br>
    • New User registration karein aur valid Email ID aur Mobile Number enter karein.<br>
    • Registration ke baad GOAPS portal par login karke apni Personal, Communication aur Academic details fill karein.<br>
    • Apne pasand ke Test Papers aur Examination City centres select karein.<br>
    • Clear Passport size Photograph, Signature aur Category/PwD Certificate (agar applicable ho) prescribed format me upload karein.<br>
    • Category-wise application fee ka online payment karein aur final submitted confirmation page ka PDF download karke printout nikal lein.
  </div>
